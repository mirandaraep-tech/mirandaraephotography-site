Text Message Terms

Last updated October 21, 2026

What these texts are

Miranda Rae Photography sends text messages about beach photography sessions: replies to your session request, booking confirmations, reminders and follow-ups about your photos.

How you sign up

You agree to receive texts by checking the text-message box on our booking form at mirandaraephotography.com/book. Checking the box is optional and is not required to book a session.

How often

Message frequency varies based on your request and booking.

Cost

Miranda Rae Photography never charges for texts. Most phone plans include unlimited texting, but standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

Stopping texts

Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving texts. You'll get one message confirming you've been unsubscribed, and no more texts after that. To start again, reply START.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or contact us at bookmirandarae@gmail.com or 448-219-6539.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Notice for more.
