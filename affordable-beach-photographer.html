Common questions about affordable beach photos

How much does a beach photographer cost on Florida's Gulf Coast?

With Miranda, a 30-minute beach mini session is $130 at Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas and Panama City Beach. In Destin, 30A and St. George Island, the Beach Special is $199. Longer sessions start at $225.

What's included in the $130 mini session?

30 minutes with Miranda, 15 professionally edited photos and room for up to 6 people, delivered in a private online gallery.

Is there an affordable family photographer in Destin or 30A?

Yes. Miranda's $199 Beach Special covers Destin, 30A and St. George Island: 30 minutes, 25 edited photos and up to 11 people.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The price you see is the session price. Once Miranda confirms your date, a $50 deposit holds it, and that deposit comes off your session price.

Do I have to pay to check my date?

No. Checking availability is free. Send your preferred date and Miranda will reply personally.

Can I see Miranda's photos before I ask?

Yes. You can browse her gallery anytime at mirandaraephotography.com/gallery.
