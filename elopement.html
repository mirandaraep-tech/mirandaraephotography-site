Beach elopement questions

How much does a beach elopement photographer cost?

Miranda's Beach Elopement is $549. It includes 2 hours and 100+ edited photos: your ceremony, family photos and sunset portraits, plus help choosing the spot and timing and finding an officiant.

Can Miranda help us find an officiant?

Yes. Miranda can help you find a local officiant who does beach ceremonies and line up the timing with them. Your officiant is booked and paid separately.

Can we book a regular photo session for our ceremony?

Ceremonies are booked as the Beach Elopement or one of Miranda's wedding packages, so there's enough time for the ceremony, your family photos and your portraits. The shorter portrait sessions don't include a ceremony.

Should we get married at sunset or sunrise?

Most couples pick sunset for the warm, golden light. Miranda plans the timing so your portraits land in the best light. Sunrise is just as pretty and the beach is much quieter, if you'd like more privacy.

Which beaches can we elope on?

Panama City Beach, Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas, Indian Pass, St. George Island, Destin and 30A, including Santa Rosa Beach, Seaside, Rosemary Beach and WaterColor. Not sure where? Miranda can help you pick a spot.

What if the weather doesn't cooperate?

Just reach out. Miranda will do her best to find a new date or time that works for you.

How do we hold our date?

Send your date with the form above. Once Miranda confirms, a $50 deposit holds your date, and it comes off your price.
