Common Questions

Why is the sunrise session cheaper?

Sunrise slots are requested less often than sunset, so we're passing the savings on to you — same 15 edited photos, same quality, just an earlier start.

What time do we need to arrive?

Sunrise time shifts a little through the year, so we'll text you the exact meeting time a few days before your session.

What should we wear?

Neutral or muted tones — soft blues, whites, tans, and sandy neutrals — photograph best against the beach. Busy patterns, logos, and bright white sneakers tend to distract from the shot.

What happens if it's cloudy or rains?

We'll work with you to reschedule for the next available sunrise session at no extra charge — you won't lose your spot to bad weather.

Do you travel to a specific spot on the beach?

Yes — we'll recommend a great public-access spot in Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, or Cape San Blas based on the tide and light that morning, or you're welcome to request a specific location.
