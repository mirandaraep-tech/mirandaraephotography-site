Questions

Which beaches do you photograph weddings on?

Miranda photographs weddings all along Florida’s Gulf Coast, including Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas, Panama City Beach, Destin, 30A and St. George Island. Beach, garden, chapel or backyard, she comes to you.

How much is wedding photography?

There are two wedding collections: the Étoile Collection is $999 for 6 hours, and the Grand Coastal Collection is $1,999 for 10 hours, from getting ready through your send-off.

How many photos will we receive?

There’s no set limit. Your gallery is curated around the story of your day, and every image that meets Miranda’s standards is professionally edited and delivered in a private online gallery.

Do I pay anything to check my date?

No. Asking is free. Miranda confirms availability and details with you first, and nothing is booked until you agree.

What if the weather turns?

Gulf weather can change fast. Miranda will talk through timing and a backup plan with you before the day.
