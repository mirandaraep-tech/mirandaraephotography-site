Miranda's tips for Destin

Know before your Destin session

01

Pick sunset or sunrise

Sunset is the favorite: soft golden light and that famous emerald water. Sunrise is just as pretty, cooler and much quieter, and great for little ones who wake up early.

02

Beat the crowds

Destin beaches get busy, especially in summer and on holiday weeks. Sunset and early-morning sessions keep the background clear of umbrellas and beach chairs.

03

Meet near where you stay

Tell Miranda your condo, rental or the beach access closest to you. Shooting close to home means less hauling kids around and more time for photos.

04

What to wear

Pick two or three soft colors that go together (whites, creams, light blues, blush or sage) instead of matching exactly. They pop against Destin's white sand and green water.

05

Plan for the wind

Gulf breezes pick up in the afternoon. Flowy dresses look amazing in the wind, but bring a hair tie or two and skip big hats.

06

Bare feet are best

Leave the shoes by the dune walkover. By sunset the sand has cooled off, and sandy toes look natural in photos.

Destin beach photographer questions

How much does a beach photographer cost in Destin?

Miranda's Beach Special is $199 for 45–60 minutes, 30–35 edited photos and up to 11 people. Destin sessions add a $30 travel fee. Bigger groups can book the $250 Family Session for up to 15 people.

What is the best time for beach photos in Destin?

The hour before sunset is the favorite, with golden light and glowing emerald water. Sunrise is a close second: cooler, calmer and almost empty beaches. Midday sun is harsh and the beaches are crowded, so Miranda books most Destin sessions at sunset or sunrise.

Can you meet us at our condo or rental in Destin?

Usually, yes. Tell Miranda where you're staying in the form, and she'll suggest the closest beach access that works well for photos.

How far ahead should we book our Destin photos?

As soon as you know your vacation dates. Sunset spots during summer and holiday weeks go first. Miranda can sometimes fit in last-minute sessions, so it never hurts to ask.

What should we wear for Destin beach photos?

Soft, coordinating colors look best, like whites, creams, light blues, blush or sage. Flowy dresses and linen work great in the breeze. Skip loud logos and matching everyone exactly.

Our kids won't sit still. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Miranda's sessions are relaxed and full of movement, so wiggly toddlers and shy kids are welcome. Some of the best photos come from the in-between moments.

Do you photograph couples in Destin too?

Yes. Couples, anniversaries and just-the-two-of-you vacation photos are welcome on the Beach Special. Planning a proposal or a small beach wedding? Miranda has separate proposal, elopement and wedding packages.

What if the weather doesn't cooperate?

Just reach out. Miranda will do her best to find a new date that works for you during your stay.

How do we hold our date?

Send your date with the form above. Once Miranda confirms, a $50 deposit holds your date, and it comes off your session price.

Do you photograph other beaches near Destin?

Yes. Miranda also photographs 30A (Santa Rosa Beach, Seaside, Rosemary Beach and WaterColor), Panama City Beach, Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas and St. George Island.
