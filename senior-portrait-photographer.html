Senior portrait questions

How much are senior pictures on the beach?

Miranda's Senior Portrait Session is $199. It includes 45–60 minutes, 30–35 edited photos, 2 outfit changes, your props, a few photos with a parent or best friend, and yearbook-ready picks marked for you.

What should my senior bring?

Two outfits (a dressy look and a casual one) and anything that shows who they are: a cap and gown, a letterman jacket, a sports jersey, an instrument or class-year balloons. Barefoot is perfect on the beach.

Can a parent or friend be in some photos?

Yes. A parent, a sibling or a best friend can join for a few photos at the end of the session, at no extra charge.

When should we take senior pictures?

Most seniors take them in the fall or early spring, so the photos are ready for the yearbook, graduation announcements and party invitations. Sunset gives the warmest light; sunrise is quieter and cooler.

Which beaches do you photograph?

Panama City Beach, Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas, Indian Pass, St. George Island, Destin and 30A, including Santa Rosa Beach, Seaside, Rosemary Beach and WaterColor. Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas and Panama City Beach have no travel fee. Destin, 30A and St. George Island add a $30 travel fee.

Who does Miranda talk to?

Miranda texts the parent who sends the request, so you stay in the loop on dates, outfits and the deposit.

How do we hold our date?

Send your date with the form above. Once Miranda confirms, a $50 deposit holds your date, and it comes off your price.
