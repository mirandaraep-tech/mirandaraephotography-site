Privacy Notice

Last updated October 8, 2026

This notice explains what happens to the information you send Miranda Rae Photography through this website.

What we collect

When you fill out a form, we receive what you type in: your name, phone number, email address, preferred date, beach or location, who will be in the photos, how you'd like Miranda to reply, and any message you add. The form also notes which page you used and how you found the site (for example, a Google search or an ad).

How it's sent

Form messages are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit, an outside form service. FormSubmit passes your message along to Miranda Rae Photography by email.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your request, check availability, and plan and deliver your session. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Website analytics and ads

This site uses Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand how visitors find us and which pages are helpful. These tools may use cookies and collect general information like the pages you visit and the type of device you use.

Questions or removal requests

To ask about your information or have it deleted, email bookmirandarae@gmail.com or text 448-219-6539.
