Affordable beach photos

What's a beach mini session?

A beach mini session is a shorter, relaxed photo session on the sand. It's an affordable way to get professional beach photos without giving up a whole evening of your vacation. It's perfect for little ones with short attention spans, couples, or a quick yearly family picture.

Miranda Rae spends 20–30 minutes with you at one spot on the beach, lightly guiding you so nobody has to know how to pose. You get 15 professionally edited photos in a private online gallery, ready for your walls, Christmas cards and the grandparents.

Beach mini session questions

How much is a beach mini session?

Miranda's Beach Mini Session is $130. It includes 20–30 minutes at one spot on the beach, up to 6 people and 15 professionally edited photos in a private online gallery.

Which beaches offer the mini session?

Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas and Panama City Beach. In Destin, 30A and St. George Island, Miranda offers the $199 Beach Special instead.

What's the difference between the Mini and the Beach Special?

Time, photos and group size. The Mini is 20–30 minutes with 15 edited photos for up to 6 people. The Beach Special is $199 for 45–60 minutes with 30–35 edited photos, up to 11 people and an outfit change.

We have more than 6 people. Can we still book a mini?

For bigger groups, the $199 Beach Special gives you more time and room for everyone. Just send your date and how many people, and Miranda will help you pick the right session.

What time of day is best?

Sunset is the favorite, with soft golden light and warm colors. Sunrise is just as pretty, cooler and quieter, and a great pick for little ones who wake up early or if your sunset date is taken.

Can I get a last-minute mini session?

Miranda can sometimes fit in last-minute sessions — it never hurts to ask.

What if the weather doesn't cooperate?

Just reach out. Miranda will do her best to find a new date or time that works for you.

How do I hold my date?

Send your date with the form above. Once Miranda confirms, a $50 deposit holds your date, and it comes off your price.
