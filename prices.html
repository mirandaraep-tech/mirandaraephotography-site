Questions about prices

What if we have more people than the package includes?

No problem. Each extra person over a package's limit is $15. For a big group, the Family Session covers up to 15 people for $250.

Can we get more edited photos?

Yes. Extra edited photos are $10 each. Just tell Miranda how many you would like.

Is there a travel fee?

Only for the farther beaches: $30 for Destin, 30A and St. George Island. Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas and Panama City Beach have no travel fee.

How do I hold my date?

Send your beach and dates and Miranda will confirm personally. Once she does, a $50 deposit holds your date, and it comes off your session price.

Ready to check your date?

Free to ask. Miranda will personally confirm availability.
