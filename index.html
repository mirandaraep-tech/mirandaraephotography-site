Questions families ask

How much does a beach photographer cost in Mexico Beach or Port St. Joe?

Beach mini sessions in Mexico Beach, Port St. Joe, Cape San Blas and Panama City Beach are $130 for 30 minutes and 15 edited photos, for up to 6 people.

How much is a family beach session in Destin or 30A?

Destin and 30A sessions are $199 for 30 minutes and 20 edited photos, for up to 11 people.

Do you photograph beach weddings?

Yes. Beach wedding photography is $499 for up to 4 hours — ceremony, family photos and shoreline portraits, delivered in a private online gallery.

What time of day is best for beach photos?

Sunset and sunrise give the softest light, but Miranda photographs any time of day based on availability. Tell her what you'd like and she'll suggest the best time for your beach.

What if we don't know how to pose?

You don't need to. Sessions are relaxed and lightly guided, with room to walk, talk and just enjoy being together.

Do I pay anything to check my date?

No. Checking your date is free and doesn't commit you to anything. Nothing is reserved until Miranda confirms the details with you.

What if the weather turns?

If Gulf weather is unsafe or unworkable, Miranda will contact you about rescheduling.
