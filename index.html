Common Questions

What should we wear?

Neutral or muted tones — soft blues, whites, tans, and sandy neutrals — photograph best against the beach. Busy patterns, logos, and bright white sneakers tend to distract from the shot.

What happens if it rains?

We'll work with you to reschedule for the next available sunset session at no extra charge — you won't lose your spot to bad weather.

How long until we get our photos?

Your 15 edited photos are delivered within 5–7 days via a private online gallery.

Do you travel to a specific spot on the beach?

Yes — we'll recommend a great public-access spot along Destin or 30A based on the crowds and lighting that day, or you're welcome to request a specific location.
